# JudiciAI Demo Video Script

**Duration:** 60-90 seconds (viral-style, fast-paced)
**Voice:** Murch (cloned, slightly sped up, high energy)
**Style:** Screen recording with voiceover

---

## Shot 1: Hook (0-8s)

*[Screen: JudiciAI landing page, clean and bright]*

**Voice:** "Your boss just told you keto lowers cholesterol. Your doctor says the opposite. Who's right? Let's find out — in under 60 seconds."

## Shot 2: The Claim (8-15s)

*[Type: "Keto can lower your cholesterol" → click Verify]*
*[Show the ⚖️ scales bouncing — "Weighing the evidence..."]*

**Voice:** "Type any claim. JudiciAI searches the web, pulls real sources, and classifies each one — FOR, AGAINST, or NEUTRAL."

## Shot 3: The Verdict (15-28s)

*[Verdict appears: MURKY, confidence 5/10]*
*[Scroll to show sources with credibility scores]*

**Voice:** "Verdict: MURKY. Confidence 5 out of 10. The evidence is genuinely split. And unlike ChatGPT, we don't pretend to know when the science doesn't. Every source is cited. Every score is explained. Click through and verify yourself."

## Shot 4: Bull & Bear (28-38s)

*[Click 🐂 Bull Case — green card appears]*
*[Click 🐻 Bear Case — red card appears]*

**Voice:** "Want both sides? Hit the bull for the strongest case FOR. Hit the bear for the strongest case AGAINST. Steel-man both arguments in seconds."

## Shot 5: Voice + Share (38-50s)

*[Click play button — Murch's voice reads the verdict]*
*[Click share → X/LinkedIn/Email buttons]*

**Voice:** "It reads the verdict out loud. Share it with your team. Copy it. Post it. The evidence speaks."

## Shot 6: Close (50-60s)

*[Quick montage: type "Red wine is good for your heart" → MURKY | "The Great Wall is visible from space" → UNSUPPORTED]*

**Voice:** "JudiciAI. Built with ElevenLabs voice and Firecrawl search. Because the world doesn't need another AI that's always confident. It needs one that's always honest."

*[End card: "JudiciAI — The evidence speaks" | @firecrawl @elevenlabs #ElevenHacks]*

## Production Notes

- **Speed:** 1.1-1.2x on Murch's voice for energy
- **Music:** Light, professional, not distracting (optional)
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts, no fancy effects
- **Key moments to nail:** The MURKY verdict reveal, the bull/bear click, the voice playback
- **Make sure to show:** Firecrawl search happening, ElevenLabs voice playing, source cards with credibility scores
